Our Story

The Coastal Spoon was born from a simple belief: the finest ingredients, treated with respect, need nothing more than honest cooking. Nestled in the vibrant Blue Area of Islamabad, we bring the flavours of the Arabian Sea straight to your plate.

Every dish is crafted daily using sustainably sourced seafood and locally grown produce, celebrating the rich culinary heritage of Pakistan's coastline.

  • ✦ Sustainably sourced seafood
  • ✦ Locally grown produce
  • ✦ Authentic coastal flavors
